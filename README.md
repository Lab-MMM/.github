This organization is dedicated to hosting and sharing code, data, and teaching materials produced by the Laboratory of Macroecology, Macroevolution and Quantitative Methods at the [Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)](https://www.ufmg.br/), Brazil.

Our main current research interests are:

1. **Developing computational tools** to identify and quantify patterns of local coexistence using the fossil record.
2. **Investigating the drivers of macroevolutionary dynamics**, with a particular focus on how dispersal and geographic context influence the speciation and extinction of lineages.
3. **Understanding the assembly of small-range lineages and endemisms** through macroevolutionary processes acting over deep time.
